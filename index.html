A Guild’s Tale Privacy Policy

Massassa Studio · Effective 13 November 2026

This policy describes A Guild’s Tale as distributed through the Google Play internal test.

Playing on this device

You can play without signing in. Your current Journey, preferences, Field Guide discoveries and Our Tales records are saved on your device. Your operating system may back up app data according to its settings.

Optional Google sign-in

If you choose to sign in, Google and Firebase Authentication process your Google account information, including your email address, account identifier and available basic profile information. The game uses your verified email to check tester access and your account identifier to manage your online account. The server keeps session information and any existing online Journey and profile associated with that account. The current Android build saves ordinary gameplay on your device.

Security and service providers

Google Firebase and Google Play Integrity process information such as IP address, app and device information, and integrity tokens to authenticate users, verify the app and prevent abuse. Render hosts the game service and processes requests needed to operate it. Online requests are encrypted in transit. The game has no ads or real-money purchases.

Request account deletion

In A Guild’s Tale, sign in and choose Google account → Delete account. You can also request deletion by email. Include the Google email you used for the game so we can locate and verify the account. Keep your password and verification codes private.

The studio keeps online account data while the account is active. After a verified deletion request, the studio removes the game’s Firebase authentication record, online profile, any server Journey and session history. A minimal deletion record is kept to prevent an old session from restoring the account. Deleting the game account does not delete your Google account. To remove your local Journey and settings, clear the game’s storage on your device.

Contact

For privacy questions or to request deletion of support correspondence, email puredalek@gmail.com. We use information you send to respond to your request and keep correspondence as needed to handle it.
